## Before
[Ep 12 — Toward the First Cities](12%20Toward%20the%20First%20Cities.md) leaves a changing material and social landscape. The opening asks what endured, what shifted, and whose work remains visible before Harappa.

## The turn
Changing streets, crafts, food systems and burials reveal an Indus metropolis, not a known capital. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## After
Consequences remain regional rather than uniform. Open questions pass to [Ep 14 — Mohenjo-daro](14%20Mohenjo-daro.md), where a different archive changes what can responsibly be said.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Brick robbing, early excavation and unread inscriptions remove crucial context. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that Harappa was the sole capital.
- Do not claim that the granary function is certain.
- Do not claim that Cemetery H proves invasion.
- Do not claim that seals give royal names.
