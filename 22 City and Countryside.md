## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Mobile camps and gendered rural labor are systematically under-recorded. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that cities produced all food.
- Do not claim that surplus was certainly tax.
- Do not claim that pastoralists stood outside society.
- Do not claim that granary functions are known.
