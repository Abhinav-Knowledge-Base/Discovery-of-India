## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Workshop evidence rarely fixes artisan gender, status or freedom. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that all artisans were enslaved.
- Do not claim that standards mean machines.
- Do not claim that craft had fixed modern genders.
- Do not claim that elite goods show the whole economy.
