## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Rice status, site seasonality and uneven preservation remain disputed. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that rice proves domestication.
- Do not claim that foragers were socially simple.
- Do not claim that burials reveal caste.
- Do not claim that all farming had one source.
