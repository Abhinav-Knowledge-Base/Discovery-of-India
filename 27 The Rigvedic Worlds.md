## The turn
Orally composed hymns evoke rivers, cattle, ritual and patronage through layered poetry, not chronicle. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Philology dates layers approximately, and archaeology cannot be matched verse by verse. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that Rigveda is a chronicle.
- Do not claim that arya is a biological race.
- Do not claim that every battle has a site.
- Do not claim that later caste rules were unchanged.
