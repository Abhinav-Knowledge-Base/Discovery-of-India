## The turn
Regional centers, craft districts and standards show Indus urbanism assembled over centuries. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Walls may indicate defense, flood control or authority differently by site. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that one capital planned all cities.
- Do not claim that urbanism arrived suddenly.
- Do not claim that standards prove political unity.
- Do not claim that walls always mean war.
