## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. One partial skull and uncertain depositional history limit biography. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that the fossil is certainly modern human.
- Do not claim that nearby tools belonged to it.
- Do not claim that one skull is an ethnic ancestor.
- Do not claim that the valley was isolated.
