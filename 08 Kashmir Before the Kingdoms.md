## The turn
Pit dwellings, mixed farming and burials reveal a connected yet distinctive prehistoric Kashmir. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Chronology and meanings of animal interments remain uncertain. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that pit dwellers were one named people.
- Do not claim that animal graves have one meaning.
- Do not claim that similar pots prove migration.
- Do not claim that Kashmir was isolated.
