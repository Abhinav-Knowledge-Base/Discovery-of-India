## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Poem dates, port identifications and polity boundaries remain contested. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that poems are eyewitness chronicles.
- Do not claim that Roman pots mean colonies.
- Do not claim that Tamil identity was timeless.
- Do not claim that every name identifies a king.
