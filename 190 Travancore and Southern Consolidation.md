## Before

Venad operated amid matrilineal houses, temples, chiefs, cultivators, and Indian Ocean trade.

## The turn

Marthanda Varma defeated rivals and the Dutch, reorganized armed power, and dedicated the kingdom to Padmanabha.

## After

Company alliance and administration preserved the state while caste hierarchy and extraction structured subjects' lives.

## Act I - Venad to Travancore

Succession disputes and campaigns brought southern Kerala under a stronger court.

## Act II - Colachel

Local strategy and resources defeated a Dutch expedition in 1741.

## Act III - Temple and kingship

Dedication to Padmanabha joined ritual sovereignty to practical rule.

## Act IV - Land, labor, caste

Pepper, taxes, bonded labor, and restrictions underwrote consolidation.

## Act V - Alliance after Mysore

War and a subsidiary arrangement narrowed autonomy.

## Subcontinental note

Travancore crosses present Kerala and Tamil Nadu and connects Malabar, the Gulf of Mannar, Sri Lanka, and European trade.

## How we know

Malayalam records, temple accounts, Dutch and Company papers, missionary texts, forts, canals, and oral traditions require comparison.

## Do not claim

- Colachel ended Dutch activity throughout Asia.
- Consolidation benefited all groups equally.
- Temple sovereignty erased royal power.
- Kerala's later borders already existed.
