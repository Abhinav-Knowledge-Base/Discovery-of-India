## The turn
Urban contraction involved climate, rivers, trade and local choices while communities persisted. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Causes, dates and experiences differed sharply by region. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that one drought destroyed everything.
- Do not claim that invaders burned every city.
- Do not claim that all Harappans moved east.
- Do not claim that urban decline means disappearance.
