# Ep 105 — Odisha Before the Gajapatis

[Series README](README.md)

## Treatment
| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | Kalinga, Utkala, and Kosala become overlapping regional worlds through Bhauma-Kara, Somavamshi, and Eastern Ganga power long before the Gajapatis. |
| **Years** | c. 700–1238 CE |
| **Before–after** | Post-Gupta eastern polities → Eastern Ganga consolidation and Jagannath-centered kingship |

## Before
Coastal plains, river valleys, forested uplands, ports, Buddhist sites, Shaiva centers, and many communities never formed one timeless Odisha.

## The turn
Bhauma-Kara queens and successors, Somavamshis, and Eastern Gangas weave grants, temples, warfare, and regional cults into changing states.

## After
Anangabhima III’s order anticipates later Gajapati idioms, but fifteenth-century empire should not be projected backward.

## Act I — Regions before a name
Map Kalinga, Utkala, Odra, and Dakshina Kosala as shifting labels rather than aliases for modern boundaries.

## Act II — Queens of the Bhauma-Karas
Study Tribhuvana Mahadevi and other women rulers while distinguishing dynastic opportunity from ordinary gender conditions.

## Act III — Temple city
Follow Bhubaneswar’s builders, stone supply, water, Shaiva institutions, and multiple patrons across centuries.

## Act IV — Forest and coast
Place agrarian expansion, Adivasi and forest communities, elephants, minerals, ports, and inland routes in one contested landscape.

## Act V — Jagannath and the Gangas
Treat Puri’s cult as a layered formation involving royal, temple, local, and possibly tribal traditions, not a single-origin story.

## Subcontinental note
Odisha connected Bengal, Andhra, Chhattisgarh, Sri Lankan and Southeast Asian sea routes while retaining its own multiple regional histories.

## How we know
Copper plates, temple inscriptions, architecture, sculpture, archaeology, ritual chronicles, and later legends are abundant but rarely represent subaltern voices.

## Do not claim
- Do not project modern Odisha unchanged into early geographic names.
- Do not infer general gender equality from Bhauma-Kara queens.
- Do not assign Jagannath one uncontested tribal or textual origin.
- Do not describe forest lands as empty royal frontiers.
- Do not call all monumental architecture a product of one dynasty.
