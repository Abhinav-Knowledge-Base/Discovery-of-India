## Item intake fields

Every candidate receives: archive ID; repository; collection/series/file/item; creator; date/range; original caption; language; format; physical/digital condition; provenance; depicted people/place; episode/timecode; claim supported; restrictions; copyright owner; underlying rights; privacy/consent; cultural protocol; licence terms; fee; credit; expiry; researcher; verification date; and status.

## Status gates

`Lead` → `Catalogue verified` → `Item viewed` → `Provenance checked` → `Rights owner confirmed` → `Terms requested` → `Licensed` → `Delivered` → `Used/credited`.

Until a signed licence or documented permission reaches `Licensed`, the rights field remains **TBD**. “Public domain,” “government work,” “orphan,” “Creative Commons,” “online,” “educational,” and “fair dealing/use” are legal assertions requiring recorded review, not informal status labels.
