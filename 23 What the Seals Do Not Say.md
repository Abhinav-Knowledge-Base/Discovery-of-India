## The turn
Structured but brief inscriptions preserve signs without yielding an accepted decipherment. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. No accepted bilingual, long text or verified reading exists. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that the script is deciphered.
- Do not claim that a few matches prove a language.
- Do not claim that animals are known gods or castes.
- Do not claim that short texts prove no literacy.
