## The turn
Territorial communities, fortified centers and varied governments prepared the way for cities and states. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## After
The episode hands onward to the mahajanapadas, second urbanization and renunciant traditions, where material and textual archives multiply without becoming identical.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Texts, ceramics and fortifications overlap imperfectly and do not draw precise borders. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that sixteen states existed unchanged.
- Do not claim that boundaries matched modern states.
- Do not claim that all were monarchies.
- Do not claim that coins identify ethnic peoples.
