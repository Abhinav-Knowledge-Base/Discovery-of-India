## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Disturbance, sample size and palaeoriver dating demand caution. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that it is conclusively largest.
- Do not claim that one genome represents all.
- Do not claim that genes reveal language.
- Do not claim that one channel is an unchanged Vedic river.
