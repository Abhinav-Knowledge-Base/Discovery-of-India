## The turn

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Epic correlations rely on texts compiled and revised much later. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that PGW people were Pandavas or Aryans.
- Do not claim that a flood proves an epic.
- Do not claim that iron alone made states.
- Do not claim that fine pots represent all households.
