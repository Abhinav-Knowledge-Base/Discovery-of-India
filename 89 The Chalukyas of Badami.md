# Ep 89 — The Chalukyas of Badami

[Series README](README.md)

## Treatment
| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Logline** | From Vatapi, the Chalukyas build a Deccan power through warfare, local alliances, and experimental temple landscapes. |
| **Years** | c. 540–757 CE |
| **Before–after** | Kadamba and local Deccan orders → Badami Chalukya ascendancy and Rashtrakuta replacement |

## Before
The western Deccan held Kadamba, Ganga, Maurya of Konkan, Alupa, Nala, village, pastoral, and forest authorities connected by routes.

## The turn
Pulakeshin I fortifies Vatapi; Pulakeshin II expands through alliances and campaigns, then meets Pallava counterattack.

## After
Vikramaditya rulers restore power and sponsor temples before Dantidurga’s Rashtrakutas displace the dynasty.

## Act I — Red sandstone capital
Use Badami’s cliffs, water, fortifications, caves, and settlement to make ecology part of state formation.

## Act II — Aihole’s voice
Read Ravikirti’s 634 inscription as brilliant praise that fixes one court’s preferred geography.

## Act III — Narmada and Kanchi
Pair resistance to Harsha with defeat by Narasimhavarman, refusing a single victor’s arc.

## Act IV — Building experimentation
Compare Aihole, Badami, Pattadakal, and Mahakuta without forcing “northern” and “southern” styles into pure boxes.

## Act V — Land and intermediaries
Trace grants, guilds, queens, officers, cultivators, and forest-edge communities while keeping feudalism a debated model.

## Subcontinental note
Chalukya history connects Gujarat, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Andhra, and Tamil regions, but none should be presented as annexed cultural margins.

## How we know
Prashastis, grants, monuments, inscriptions by queens, coins, and archaeology are plentiful but overwhelmingly elite and commemorative.

## Do not claim
- Do not treat Ravikirti’s conquest list as an administrative map.
- Do not make Pulakeshin II’s death date or circumstances more certain than evidence.
- Do not call temple forms racially “Aryan” or “Dravidian.”
- Do not reduce grants to simple feudal fragmentation.
- Do not attribute monuments only to kings when queens, guilds, and artisans appear.

## Figures, sites, and works
| Item | Why it matters |

## Locations and visual evidence
Film Badami, Aihole, Pattadakal, Mahakuta, inscription surfaces, quarries, and Malaprabha fields; animate campaigns as dated claims.
