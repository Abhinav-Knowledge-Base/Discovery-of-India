## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Absolute chronology and links to language communities stay uncertain. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that Cemetery H equals Aryans.
- Do not claim that Punjab became empty.
- Do not claim that pottery proves language.
- Do not claim that Rigveda describes ruins directly.
