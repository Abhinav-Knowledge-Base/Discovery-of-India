## The turn
Stone architecture, reservoirs and public signs show local ingenuity within Harappan practice. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Population, sign meaning and the weight of drought remain debated. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that water scarcity was permanently solved.
- Do not claim that the signboard is deciphered.
- Do not claim that reservoirs prove monarchy.
- Do not claim that drought alone ended the city.
