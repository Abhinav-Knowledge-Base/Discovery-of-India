## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Zooarchaeology, linguistics and genetics have imperfectly overlapping clocks. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that horse bones prove Vedic identity.
- Do not claim that one race invented chariots.
- Do not claim that language equals genes.
- Do not claim that one invasion explains all.
