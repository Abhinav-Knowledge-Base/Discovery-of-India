## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Meluhha's limits, languages and trade institutions remain unknown. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that Meluhha is an Indus self-name.
- Do not claim that every bead came from one city.
- Do not claim that trade proves colonization.
- Do not claim that foreign texts describe all Indus life.
