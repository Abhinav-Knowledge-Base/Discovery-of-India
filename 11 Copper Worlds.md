## Before
[Ep 10 — The Southern Neolithic](10%20The%20Southern%20Neolithic.md) leaves a changing material and social landscape. The opening asks what endured, what shifted, and whose work remains visible before Copper Worlds.

## The turn
Regional villages used copper within older material worlds and followed no single road to cities. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## After

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Culture labels and house differences cannot by themselves define peoples or classes. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that copper replaced stone.
- Do not claim that pot styles name peoples.
- Do not claim that metal inevitably made states.
- Do not claim that all villages served Harappa.
