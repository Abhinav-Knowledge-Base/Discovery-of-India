## The turn
Surveyed mounds around changing Hakra channels reveal an urban countryside, not a lost-river legend. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Few excavations prevent exact dates and functions for most surveyed sites. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that every channel is Sarasvati.
- Do not claim that one river dried at once.
- Do not claim that surface pots give exact dates.
- Do not claim that desertion means disappearance.
