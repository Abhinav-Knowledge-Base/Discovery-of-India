## Before

The Vijayanagara world connected southern Karnataka through tribute, temples, agrarian expansion, and mobile warrior elites.

## The turn

Wodeyar rulers enlarged Mysore while dalavayis and commanders gained power amid competition with Marathas and Hyderabad.

## After

Haidar Ali inherited neither a blank slate nor an uncontested throne, but a mature and disputed regional state.

## Act I - Vijayanagara ties

Early Wodeyars converted local authority into a kingdom while retaining older forms.

## Act II - Capital and court

Srirangapatna joined fortification, sacred geography, markets, and display.

## Act III - Land and water

Tanks, wet cultivation, pastoral zones, and forests sustained revenue.

## Act IV - Chiefs and commanders

Dalavayis, palegars, soldiers, and merchants negotiated variable court power.

## Act V - Military transition

Drilled infantry and artillery entered an existing southern military economy.

## Subcontinental note

Mysore interacted with Kodagu, Malabar, Tamil regions, the Carnatic, and the Deccan; frontier communities experienced expansion differently.

## How we know

Inscriptions, kaifiyats, palace records, travel accounts, temples, forts, tanks, and objects complicate dynastic narratives.

## Do not claim

- Mysore began with Haidar Ali.
- Wodeyar rule was fixed and centralized.
- Military modernization was simply imported from Europe.
- Local chiefs and cultivators lacked agency.
