## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Early phases and abandonment remain unevenly published despite rich excavation. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that Mehrgarh was a western colony.
- Do not claim that all domesticates had one origin.
- Do not claim that grave goods map fixed classes.
- Do not claim that Harappan identity already existed.
