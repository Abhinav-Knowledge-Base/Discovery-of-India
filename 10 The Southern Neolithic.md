## Before
[Ep 9 — The Ganga Before the Cities](09%20The%20Ganga%20Before%20the%20Cities.md) leaves a changing material and social landscape. The opening asks what endured, what shifted, and whose work remains visible before The Southern Neolithic.

## The turn
Ashmounds, cattle, millets and stone craft reveal communal labor across the southern Deccan. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## After
Consequences remain regional rather than uniform. Open questions pass to [Ep 11 — Copper Worlds](11%20Copper%20Worlds.md), where a different archive changes what can responsibly be said.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Ashmound ritual meanings and the scale of pastoral mobility stay open. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that ashmounds are volcanic.
- Do not claim that herding excluded farming.
- Do not claim that one crop equals one migrant people.
- Do not claim that the region was uniform.
