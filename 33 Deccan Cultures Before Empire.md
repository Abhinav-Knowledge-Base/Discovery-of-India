## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Social rank and the causal weight of aridity remain debated. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that Jorwe names an ethnicity.
- Do not claim that drought alone emptied Deccan.
- Do not claim that house burials reveal caste.
- Do not claim that villages awaited northern empires.
