## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Tombs overrepresent selected people and do not transparently reveal rank or gender. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that all tombs belong to one people.
- Do not claim that weapons always mark men.
- Do not claim that grave wealth equals rank.
- Do not claim that megaliths ended with history.
