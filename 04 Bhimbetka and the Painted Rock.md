## The turn
Layered occupation and superimposed images make Bhimbetka a changing place, not one gallery. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Most paintings lack direct dates, so style and overlap provide only relative order. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that all paintings are Palaeolithic.
- Do not claim that each scene records an event.
- Do not claim that meanings stayed unchanged.
- Do not claim that style gives exact dates.
