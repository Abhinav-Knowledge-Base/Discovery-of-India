## The turn
Ironworking spread within farming and craft systems rather than causing an instant revolution. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Preservation and disputed associations complicate first-use claims. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that iron alone cleared forests.
- Do not claim that one date settles all.
- Do not claim that iron automatically created kingdoms.
- Do not claim that smiths began as one caste.
