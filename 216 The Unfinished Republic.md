## Before

The Constitution established universal citizenship and institutions without erasing Partition, caste, patriarchy, poverty, border disputes, or regional difference.

## The turn

Elections, courts, legislatures, movements, media, universities, public systems, and local governments repeatedly renegotiated freedom and authority.

## After

Achievements and failures coexist; institutional disagreement, historical evidence, and future choices prevent any declaration that the republic is complete.

## Act I - Democratic depth

Mass voting, panchayats, women's participation, and regional parties widened voice while money, violence, and exclusion constrained it.

## Act II - Equality's unfinished work

Dalit, Adivasi, OBC, minority, feminist, labor, disability, and queer movements made rights real through conflict.

## Act III - Federal borderlands

Kashmir, the Northeast, linguistic regions, and center-state finance show union as continuing negotiation.

## Act IV - Economy, science, health

Growth, space and digital achievements, welfare, and medicine coexist with informal work, unequal care, and knowledge disputes.

## Act V - Environment and future

Air, water, heat, forests, coasts, energy, and climate justice connect local livelihoods to planetary risk.

## Subcontinental note

India's histories remain entangled with Pakistan, Bangladesh, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Bhutan, Myanmar, the diaspora, and Indian Ocean worlds.

## How we know

Election and census data, judgments, parliamentary records, budgets, scientific studies, environmental monitoring, journalism, art, oral history, and everyday archives all require scrutiny.

## Do not claim

- The republic has reached a final historical form.
- One election result expresses a permanent national will.
- Economic or technological success cancels social inequality.
- Critique proves failure, or patriotism requires silence.
