## The turn
Texts, labor, gender and donations show changing hierarchy; varna is not timeless jati. The camera centers labor, food, gender and environment, then labels the move from observation to inference.

## After

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Elite texts privilege projects of order; local jati histories are later and uneven. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that varna and jati are identical.
- Do not claim that four varnas described everyone.
- Do not claim that caste has one origin author.
- Do not claim that genes assign ancient caste.
