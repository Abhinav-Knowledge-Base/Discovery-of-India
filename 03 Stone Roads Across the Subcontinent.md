## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Dating and tephra anchor sequences, but tools do not identify populations. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that prepared cores prove Homo sapiens.
- Do not claim that Toba emptied all regions.
- Do not claim that similar tools prove one people.
- Do not claim that movement followed one arrow.
