## The turn

## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Preservation obscures household work and the threshold of domestication. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that villages appeared at once.
- Do not claim that farming instantly replaced foraging.
- Do not claim that cultivation proves domestication.
- Do not claim that settlement guaranteed equality.
