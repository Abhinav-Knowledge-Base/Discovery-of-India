## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Old excavation, selective graves and limited DNA constrain synthesis. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that the graves equal Aryan invaders.
- Do not claim that objects state language.
- Do not claim that genes pots and texts align.
- Do not claim that all change used one pass.
