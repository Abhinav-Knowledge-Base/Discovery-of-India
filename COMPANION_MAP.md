## Status vocabulary

- **Identity verified:** series and episode count are established; exact catalogue metadata still needs production verification unless separately stated.
- **Mapping provisional:** thematic correspondence is useful but must be checked against the viewed episode.
- **Access TBD:** no durable authorized viewing location is asserted.
- **Rights TBD:** inclusion of clips, stills, scripts, music or publicity material is not cleared.

## Verification workflow

1. Assign a stable companion ID; record producer, year, language, runtime and episode title from a primary catalogue.
2. View the master, not a compilation or unverified upload.
3. Log exact timecode, content, original source within the program and the claim it would support.
4. Fact-check independently; a companion program is a secondary interpretation.
5. Identify copyright owner, physical custodian and licensing representative separately.
6. Mark **verified** only when catalogue metadata and viewed content agree.
7. Mark **cleared** only after signed permission states media, territory, term, platforms and promotional use.
