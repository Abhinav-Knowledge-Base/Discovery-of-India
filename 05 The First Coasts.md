## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Inundation and tropical decay erase most early coastal evidence. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that a continuous coastal trail survives.
- Do not claim that routes are precisely known.
- Do not claim that seafood caused human modernity.
- Do not claim that islanders are unchanged survivors.
