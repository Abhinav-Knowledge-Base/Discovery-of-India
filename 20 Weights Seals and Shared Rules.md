## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Unread records conceal who enforced conventions and at what scale. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that weights were coins.
- Do not claim that seals are deciphered passports.
- Do not claim that bricks prove an emperor.
- Do not claim that unicorns have a known religious meaning.
