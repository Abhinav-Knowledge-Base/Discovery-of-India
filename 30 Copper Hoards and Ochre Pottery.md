## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Chance discoveries create severe contextual and chronological gaps. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that hoards belonged to one warrior race.
- Do not claim that swords prove chariot armies.
- Do not claim that OCP is one ethnicity.
- Do not claim that ore sources map migration.
