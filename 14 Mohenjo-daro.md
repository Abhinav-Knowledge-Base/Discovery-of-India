## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Early recording, salinity and unread writing constrain political interpretation. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that the Bath was certainly a temple.
- Do not claim that the Priest-King ruled.
- Do not claim that one flood ended the city.
- Do not claim that drains equal modern public health.
