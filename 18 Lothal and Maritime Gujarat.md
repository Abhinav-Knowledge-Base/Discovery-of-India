## Subcontinental note
The frame is the historical subcontinent, not modern-border nationalism. Pakistan, India and Afghanistan enter where sites, rivers and routes connect them; local agency and regional difference remain visible.

## How we know
The episode combines archaeological contexts, environmental proxies and, where relevant, textual, epigraphic or biological evidence. Ancient hydrology allows competing interpretations of the basin. Evidence, inference and later memory receive separate labels.

## Do not claim
- Do not claim that the basin is certainly the first dock.
- Do not claim that Lothal controlled all sea trade.
- Do not claim that a seal proves ethnicity.
- Do not claim that flood alone ended settlement.
